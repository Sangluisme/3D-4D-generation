**Gaps** (empty rows worth noting, not omissions):
- **Flow-matching**: no paper surveyed uses it as the primary sampler for full 4D asset/scene generation — an active area in 3D, essentially unclaimed in 4D. It shows up only in skeletal *motion*-only generation (FlowMotion, Riemannian Motion Generation), which is out of scope here since those don't generate appearance/texture.
- **GAN**: no notable GAN-based 4D generation papers turned up — 4D generation emerged as a field mostly after diffusion became dominant.
